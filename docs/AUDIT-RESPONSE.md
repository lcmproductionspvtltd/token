# Response to the BQuee code, security and deployment audit (19 Nov 2026)

Each finding and section of the audit, what was changed, and the test that proves it. "Outside code" items
need a person or a service (a penetration tester, a lawyer, the hosting account); they are listed at the end.

## Findings

| #             | Finding                                | What was done                                                                                                                        | Proved by                                                                       |

## Outside code (needs a person or a service)

- **Penetration test** by an independent tester before selling to a client.
- **Legal**: privacy policy, terms, data processing agreement, deletion/export promises, breach-notice plan.
- **Hosting**: paid database with backups and point-in-time recovery; uptime monitor; Cloudflare in front.
- **E-mail**: SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your sending domain (phishing protection).
- **Apps**: release signing keys, store privacy forms, testing on real phones.
- **Commercial**: licences of included parts (Flutter's material_ui/cupertino_ui are BSD), support and uptime promises.
